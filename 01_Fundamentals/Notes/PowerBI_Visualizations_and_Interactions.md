# Power BI Visualizations and Interactions

## Basic Charts

Power BI provides several chart types for data analysis.

### Common Visuals

* Column Chart
* Bar Chart
* Line Chart
* Pie Chart
* Donut Chart
* Card Visual
* Table
* Matrix

### Best Practice

Choose the chart type based on the business question and data structure.

---

## Chart Customization

Visuals can be customized using the Format Pane.

### Common Formatting Options

* Titles
* Colors
* Data Labels
* Backgrounds
* Borders
* Axes
* Tooltips

Proper formatting improves readability and storytelling.

---

## Visual Interactions

Power BI visuals interact with one another automatically.

### Types

* Cross Filtering
* Cross Highlighting

### Example

Selecting a country in one chart updates related visuals across the report page.

---

## Hierarchies

Hierarchies enable drill-down analysis.

### Example

Year
→ Quarter
→ Month
→ Day

### Benefits

* Detailed exploration
* Trend analysis
* Interactive reporting

---

## Card Visuals

Card visuals display a single KPI or metric.

### Examples

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Customer Count
* Revenue

### Benefits

* Quick KPI monitoring
* Executive reporting
* Dashboard summaries
