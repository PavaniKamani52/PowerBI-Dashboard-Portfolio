# Basic Power BI Visualizations Report

## Overview

This report was created as part of my Power BI learning journey to understand how different visualizations can be used to analyze business data effectively.

The dashboard focuses on sales amount and shipment analysis across countries, products, and time periods using a combination of Power BI visuals.

---

## Dashboard Objectives

The primary goal of this report is to:

- Analyze shipment distribution across countries
- Compare product-wise shipment performance
- Track sales trends over time
- Understand contribution and variance using a waterfall chart
- Practice visual selection and report layout design

---

## Visualizations Used

### 1. Column Chart – Boxes Shipped by Country

**Purpose:** Compare shipment volumes across countries.

**Insights:**
- Australia recorded the highest number of boxes shipped.
- Canada and the UK followed closely behind.
- USA and New Zealand showed comparatively lower shipment volumes.

**Skills Practiced:**
- Column Chart Creation
- Sorting Visuals
- Axis Formatting
- Data Comparison

---

### 2. Bar Chart – Boxes Shipped by Product

**Purpose:** Analyze product-level shipment performance.

**Insights:**
- 50% Dark Bites generated the highest shipment volume.
- Smooth Silky Salty and Eclairs also performed strongly.
- Product comparison becomes easier through horizontal bar charts.

**Skills Practiced:**
- Bar Chart Design
- Ranking Products
- Visual Formatting
- Category Analysis

---

### 3. Line Chart – Sales Trend Analysis

**Purpose:** Monitor sales amount over time.

**Insights:**
- Sales fluctuated throughout the year.
- Peak sales occurred during mid-year periods.
- Trend analysis helps identify seasonal patterns and business performance.

**Skills Practiced:**
- Time-Series Analysis
- Date Hierarchies
- Trend Visualization
- Interactive Reporting

---

### 4. Waterfall Chart – Contribution Analysis

**Purpose:** Understand increases and decreases contributing to overall sales.

**Insights:**
- Individual products contributed differently to total revenue.
- Positive and negative variances can be easily identified.
- Waterfall charts provide a clear view of cumulative impact.

**Skills Practiced:**
- Waterfall Charts
- Variance Analysis
- Contribution Tracking
- Business Storytelling
